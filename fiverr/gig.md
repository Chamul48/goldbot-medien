## Description (max 1200 characters)
Got a podcast, YouTube video, webinar or interview sitting on your drive? I turn it into ready-to-post vertical clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

What you get in every clip:
✔ The strongest moments, picked for a hook in the first 3 seconds
✔ Animated word-by-word captions in your brand colors
✔ Vertical 9:16 crop that keeps the speaker in frame
✔ Clean cuts – no dead air, no "ehm"
✔ A cover/thumbnail frame and a title idea for each clip

Languages: English and German (captions and translation).

How it works:
1. Send me your video (link or upload) and your brand colors/logo.
2. I deliver your clips as MP4 (1080×1920), ready to upload.
3. Not happy with a clip? Revisions are included.

Transparency: I use AI-assisted tools for transcription, clip selection and captions. Every clip is reviewed by me before delivery.

Please message me before ordering if your video is longer than 90 minutes or you need a special style – I'll make you a custom offer.

## FAQ
**Which videos work best?**
Talking-head content: podcasts, interviews, coaching calls, webinars, vlogs.

**Do you use AI?**
Yes, for transcription, finding strong moments and generating captions. I review and adjust every clip by hand before delivery.

**Can you post the clips for me?**
No, you get the files and post them yourself, so your account stays fully in your control.

**Can I choose the moments myself?**
Sure – send timestamps and I'll cut exactly those.

**What do you need from me?**
The video (or a download link), your logo and brand colors if you have them, and the platforms you're posting on.
